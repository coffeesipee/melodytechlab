# Product Requirements Document (PRD)

## 1. Product Overview

**Product Name:** Melodytechlab
**Tagline:** Premium software development agency focused on quality and speed.
**Description:** Melodytechlab is the digital storefront and portfolio for a one-person software development agency. The agency specializes in building high-quality, modern web and mobile applications that feel premium, launch smoothly, and are highly reliable. The website serves as a primary lead generation tool, showcasing past work, detailing services offered, and providing a streamlined way for potential clients to get in touch.

## 2. Target Audience

*   **Startups & Founders:** Individuals or small teams looking for a reliable, fast-shipping technical partner to build their MVPs or scale their products.
*   **Small to Medium Businesses (SMBs):** Companies needing custom software, web applications, or mobile apps to improve their operations or digital presence.
*   **Agencies needing overflow capacity:** Larger agencies looking to subcontract high-quality web or mobile development work.

## 3. Goals & Objectives

### Business Goals
*   Establish a professional, premium brand identity for the solo agency.
*   Generate high-quality inbound leads for software development projects.
*   Showcase technical expertise through a modern, performant website and a strong portfolio.

### User Goals
*   Quickly understand what services the agency offers.
*   View tangible examples of past work (portfolio) to assess quality.
*   Easily find contact information and initiate a project inquiry.
*   Experience a fast, accessible, and visually appealing website that reflects the agency's quality standards.

## 4. Key Features & Requirements

### 4.1. Core Pages & Navigation
*   **Home Page:** Hero section with strong value proposition, brief service overview, featured portfolio items, and a clear Call to Action (CTA).
*   **About Page (`/about`):** Details about the founder, agency philosophy, and technical expertise.
*   **Portfolio Page (`/portfolio`):** A gallery of past projects with case studies detailing the problem, solution, and technologies used.
*   **Articles/Blog (`/articles`):** A section for technical writing, insights, and updates to establish authority in the field.
*   **Contact Section:** A streamlined form or direct links for potential clients to get in touch.

### 4.2. Design & User Experience (UX)
*   **Theme:** Modern, sleek, dark-mode default (as indicated by the atmospheric glowing mesh and grid background).
*   **Typography:** Clean, highly readable fonts (using Next.js Geist fonts).
*   **Responsiveness:** Fully responsive design that works flawlessly on mobile, tablet, and desktop devices.
*   **Performance:** Lightning-fast page loads (Core Web Vitals optimized).

### 4.3. Content & SEO
*   **SEO Optimization:** Proper metadata, semantic HTML, and fast loading speeds to rank well for relevant keywords (web development, mobile apps, Next.js, Supabase, React).
*   **Open Graph:** Configured OG tags and images for visually appealing social sharing.

## 5. Success Metrics

*   **Lead Conversion Rate:** Percentage of visitors who submit a contact inquiry.
*   **Traffic:** Monthly unique visitors, specifically organic search traffic.
*   **Engagement:** Average time spent on the portfolio and article pages.
*   **Performance:** Lighthouse score of 90+ across Performance, Accessibility, Best Practices, and SEO.

## 6. Future Enhancements
*   Interactive project cost estimator.
*   Client portal for active project tracking.
*   Newsletter signup for technical articles and agency updates.
